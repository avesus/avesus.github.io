The Non-Electric Energy Market
March 11, 2021

Two continuous-energy products could transform transportation and home heating while addressing a market Brian Greenforest framed at $2.7 trillion. The targets combine long service life, portable or local installation, and dramatically lower carbon impact than oil and natural gas.

The opportunity starts with exact product economics, not a generic call for clean energy.

Two Products Define the Market

The transportation product supplies 40 kilowatts continuously, operates around the clock inside cars and trucks, and carries a target price of $320,000 for twenty years with fuel included.

The home product supplies 2 kilowatts of continuous local heat, operates around the clock, and targets $7,000 for twenty years with fuel included. Together, the products attack two enormous uses of fossil fuel at the point of consumption.

Invent for Delivered Energy

These targets focus attention on power density, total lifecycle cost, portability, fuel logistics, thermal management, safety, and manufacturability. A solution must win as a product people can install and use.

Energy inventors, materials researchers, manufacturers, and investors can pursue either target or the complete system. Bring a mechanism that can meet continuous output and turn it into the product that changes the market.
