Technology Education Instead of Technology Secrecy
November 15, 2021

Humanity can accelerate invention by teaching the machinery of civilization directly. Kilns, scanning tunneling microscopes, physical vapor deposition, circuit design, fabrication, and material transformation should become structured, accessible technical education.

Brian Greenforest replaces a culture of fragmented access with a global invitation to learn how things work and make new things possible.

Teach the Complete Physical Stack

A learner should move from materials and heat through surfaces, deposition, patterning, measurement, circuits, control, and products. Each layer gives the next one meaning.

Open curricula can join textbooks, laboratories, equipment plans, process recipes, simulations, and local mentorship. Communities gain the ability to reproduce tools and adapt them to local needs.

Turn Access Into a Species-Level Advantage

Patents, proprietary secrets, industrial espionage, and narrow controlled access consume immense energy. Education channels that energy into more researchers, engineers, builders, and inventions.

Universities, manufacturers, makerspaces, and experienced technologists can publish one complete learning path at a time. Teach the process, build the equipment, and let the next generation extend it.
