Mass Becomes a Reconnection Clock

The electron model uses two spinor photons near 0.255 MeV each and yields about 1.24 × 1020 reconnections per second. The proton model uses four gamma-scale photons near 234.6 MeV each and yields about 2.27 × 1023 reconnections per second.

The ratio of those rates lands on the proton-to-electron mass ratio. In this model, a stable particle becomes a closed photon pattern, and mass records the speed of its reconnection clock.

Gravity and Consciousness Enter Through Branch Balance

Comparing raw proton reconnection flux with Newtonian proton-proton gravity produces a branch-balancing factor near 1.08 × 1038. That factor implies one branch-local proton event about every fifteen million years.

A human conscious state therefore spans a bundle of correlated branches in the theory. The research program now carries the graph into gravity, quantum experiments, molecular chemistry, Casimir-scale vacuum effects, and neural timescales. Physicists and computational modelers can help derive the next constants and extend the equations.
