Public Technology Work and Openness
March 20, 2023

Technology that asks for public attention should give the public a path to understand, use, improve, and collaborate on it. Brian Greenforest calls for open source, open hardware, and global participation as the natural operating model for public technical work.

Work that genuinely requires national-security classification belongs inside its controlled system. Public commercial technology should compete through execution, service, quality, and shared capability.

Choose a Coherent Operating Model

Open projects expose source, interfaces, hardware assumptions, contribution paths, and governance. That visibility lets customers evaluate the system and lets collaborators extend it.

Classified programs protect a different mission through explicit authority and access control. Mixing secret technical dependency with public promotion weakens accountability on both sides.

Build Capitalism Around Shared Capability

Companies can earn through products, manufacturing, integration, hosting, support, certification, and trusted delivery while the underlying knowledge remains available for scrutiny and improvement.

Founders and research leaders can open the parts that create ecosystem value, publish a serious collaboration path, and let global talent help the technology reach its full scale.
