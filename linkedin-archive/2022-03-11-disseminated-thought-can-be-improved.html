Disseminated Thought Can Be Improved
March 11, 2022

An idea begins to improve when it enters other minds. Publishing, rereading, critique, and revision turn private conviction into a shared structure that people can challenge, strengthen, and use.

Brian Greenforest presents dissemination as more than communication. It becomes an engine of collective thought and a way for people to build a better world together.

Circulation Creates Intellectual Leverage

A private notebook can preserve an intuition, yet only contact with readers reveals ambiguity, missing connections, and unexpected uses. Each conversation adds another perspective to the idea.

Repeated review matters as much as first publication. Return to the thought, sharpen its language, share it again, and let the next exchange deepen the model.

Make Thought a Public Force

Brian links this cycle to an idealist view of reality and uses decoherence as an evocative connection between possibility and material consequence. The practical instruction remains immediate: ideas gain form through interaction.

Publish the idea that matters, invite serious discussion, and revise it in the open. The next reader may supply the connection that turns thought into action.
