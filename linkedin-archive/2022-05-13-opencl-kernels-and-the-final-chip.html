OpenCL Kernels and the Final Chip
May 13, 2022

Accelerator kernels should move to the best hardware without forcing programmers to configure every device, buffer, route, and security boundary. The Final Chip architecture places and manages OpenCL-style computation across an expandable cluster of reconfigurable silicon.

A new language can make allocation, ownership, communication, and reconfiguration part of one program while the physical fabric grows from a device to a data center and beyond.

Turn Kernels Into Managed Objects

Each kernel becomes a region with explicit inputs, outputs, state, and capabilities. The runtime can place that region near data, connect it to services, replicate it for load, and change its hardware allocation as demand shifts.

Local object-capability relationships protect sensitive resources without wrapping every interaction in an unrelated software stack.

Build the Research Partnership

Microsoft’s history with FPGA acceleration, cloud infrastructure, developer platforms, and HoloLens research makes it a natural partner for this architecture. The work connects chips, language design, distributed systems, and elastic service delivery.

Research labs, cloud providers, and semiconductor partners can host, fund, or co-develop the next implementation. Bring the kernels, infrastructure, and fabrication path that can carry the Final Chip into the world.
