LLMs, Reinforcement Learning, and Physical World Models
April 10, 2023

Language prediction and scalar reward cannot carry the whole structure of an intelligent world. Machines need physical models that track objects, bonds, mass, state, higher-order properties, possible actions, and the consequences of intervention.

Brian Greenforest extends Yann LeCun’s critique of LLMs and reinforcement learning toward grounded state estimation, abductive reasoning, quantum possibility, and sentience.

Give the Machine a World With Persistent Structure

A useful world model represents entities and relationships that continue across observations. It estimates mass, contact, bonding, motion, affordances, and emergent features rather than predicting only the next token or maximizing a reward signal.

That structure lets the system test counterfactuals and choose explanations. Abduction becomes a search through physically meaningful possibilities.

Connect Physical Grounding With Conscious Agency

Brian proposes quantum sources as a way to introduce genuine alternatives into machine reasoning and connects that possibility with the branch selection living agents perform.

The program invites collaboration across robotics, machine learning, physics, neuroscience, and philosophy. Build models that can act in a persistent world and study what new forms of agency emerge.
